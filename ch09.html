Chapter 9 · Operations

Daily system and weekly review

You can leave this chapter when tomorrow’s block is on a calendar, not a mood, and you know the three numbers you will write down on Sunday.

Talent does not compound. Blocks do. If the reply block is “when I have time,” you will have time on January 1 and never again.

BlockTimeOutputSkip rule
Write15–20 minOne original, or noneSkip if it does not map to a job
Peer replies15 min8–12 Column A / CDo not skip
Reach replies15 min5–8 Column B with an addSkip if you would only cheerlead
Answer5–10 minEvery useful commentDo not skip. Mutual-reply weight.

If you only have 30 minutes, cut Write, keep Peers + Answer.

IST timing for English-speaking builders

  • Late morning: Column A / C.
  • 8–10pm: Column B. US founders post. First 15 minutes still matter.
  • Friday: scoreboard in the Write slot.
  • Sunday: review, not a new format.

The X log, five lines

  1. Date and minutes actually spent.
  2. Replies that passed the standalone test.
  3. Authors who answered.
  4. Original shipped (job name) or “none.”
  5. Profile visits and follows from Analytics.

Sunday: which reply earned a visit? Which original earned a follow? Which name goes on Column C? Which name leaves A? One kill. One keep. Ten names refreshed.

Habits that keep the system cheap

  • Track five numbers in one sheet every week: impressions, profile visits, follows, replies given, replies received. Only tracking followers optimizes for the wrong game.
  • Live posting versus scheduling is a real choice. Post live when you are riding a wave. Batch when you are travelling or busy. Neither is dogma.
  • Remix, do not copy. Scroll the top posts of the best accounts in your niche, extract the hook, then rewrite it in your own story and numbers.
  • Pick one public goal for a year and do not switch it. Show the messy process every day. A sentence like “a video a day until this hits $X” is the whole strategy.
  • Do not be so busy you have no time to think. Hustle as identity is how accounts stay at 400 for a year.
