Chapter 10 · Guardrails

Kill list, dos and don’ts

You can leave this chapter when you have crossed off every item you have done in the last 30 days and written the replacement.

Most stalled accounts are not lazy. They are busy at the wrong work. If an activity does not raise Gravity, it is a hobby. Hobbies are allowed. They are not the course.

Do

  • Treat every reply as a mini-post
  • Stay in one niche 90 days
  • Publish a weekly number, even ugly
  • Answer useful comments in 30 min
  • Log profile visits, not just follows
  • Refresh ten target names every Sunday
  • Lock photo, bio job, and pin
  • Talk to same-size accounts first

Don’t

  • Bots, pods, follow/unfollow, engagement groups
  • Paste a chatbot raw
  • Feature changelogs as content
  • Worship the 2023 13.5× table
  • Train the graph on connect threads
  • Hunt 200k accounts with “great point”
  • Swap persona at 780 because you are bored
  • Buy followers
Looks like workDo this instead
Redesigning the bannerFive-second bio test with a human
Planning 30 content pillarsPick one job for 90 days
Reading 12 growth threadsDo the Chapter 4 drill
Building a scheduling stackA calendar event named A/C block
Warm-up tools for a new altRewrite the old account

Kindness is a filter, not a brand. Quote-dunks buy reach and spend Gravity. If the only people who reply are enemies, you will not have a Column C.

Drill · 10 minutes

List three things you did last week that belong on the don’t list. Write the replacement. Put it on tomorrow’s card.
