Before you start

What still holds. What we patched.

Rob Hallam’s Attention Playbook is still in pre-sale as of 30 Sep 2026 and does not ship until 31 Oct. This course is the public synthesis, not a leak of his videos.

  1. Do not quote 2023 ranker weights as current law. The famous “reply = 13.5, author-engaged reply = 75, like = 0.5” table is from the 2023 heavy ranker. The 2026 Phoenix ranker still rewards replies, profile clicks, follows, dwell, and shares more than likes — but the exact multipliers moved. Treat the shape as durable: conversation and profile curiosity beat vanity likes.
  2. Peer replies and big-account replies are different jobs. Friends your size notice you. A specific reply under a 5k–80k niche account, posted in the first 15 minutes, borrows reach. Split 70 / 30.
  3. Speed-run screenshots are not a plan. 1 → 198 in two days, 200 → 700 in four days, 500 in three days — these usually hide an existing audience, six-hour days, or connect-thread volume. Durable cases: Luckforest (8 months, ~3,800 posts) and ClimbX (0 → 1k in 33 days only at 2–6 hours/day).

Ranker caveat: AutoTraction Sep 2026 Phoenix read; xDoctor June 2026 verification that 13.5 is historical.
