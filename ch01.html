Chapter 1 · 18 min

How X ranks a post in 2026

You can leave this chapter when you can explain why 12 thoughtful replies can outrank 80 likes, and why answering a comment in 30 minutes is not engagement bait.

X does not broadcast to your followers. Each post is a test: did this earn the next circle of viewers? Follower count is a weak prior. That is why a 200-follower account can outrun a 20k account on a Tuesday, and why six months of posting into silence is usually a reply-and-profile problem, not a luck problem.

The model, in one paragraph

Phoenix (For You, open-sourced 13 Aug 2026) predicts ~19 actions a viewer might take. Each action has a weight. The score is the weighted sum. You do not need the paper. You need the shape of the weights, because the shape tells you what to write.

Weights will move. Treat this table as a snapshot of one public release, and check the open-sourced repository before you quote a number in your own posts. Do not quote the 2023 table (reply = 13.5) as current law. The shape has held across versions: conversation, profile actions and off-platform shares beat hearts. Build your habits on the shape, not on the decimals.

Two circles

Circle one is people who already talk to you. If they reply or the author replies back, the post is allowed to leave the room.

Circle two is strangers the model is willing to test. They only show up if circle one produced a score. Beginners try to skip to circle two with a banger. The banger never leaves the room.

What this changes about your Tuesday

  1. Write so a specific person can answer. “Thoughts?” is not a reason.
  2. Sit with the post for 30–40 minutes after you hit send.
  3. Answer every useful comment as if it were a second post.
  4. Ignore vanity likes. Log profile visits and unprompted replies.
Wrong read “Replies are worth 10× likes, so I will reply ‘this!’ under 80 posts.” Cheerleading trains the graph that you are a bot.
Right read You post a 90-day scoreboard. Three peers ask a real question. You answer each with a number. That cluster is what Phoenix is built to promote.
Drill · 15 minutes

Open your last 10 posts. Write likes vs replies that were not yours. Circle high-like / zero-reply posts. Rewrite one last line so it forces a specific answer.
