Chapter 8 · Weeks 6–12

500–1,000: one format

1,000 is allowed to mean ~20 unprompted repliers, a weekly post that can clear four-figure views, and one sentence a stranger remembers. A badge from a follow-train does not count.

At 500 the temptation is to reinvent yourself. New aesthetic. New pillars. That is how 780 dies. The room finally knows what you are. Do not confuse them to feel fresh.

If a format dies three times, it is dead. Change the metric, not the entire persona. Three explainers that stall might mean they are too long. Cut them in half before you invent a newsletter.

This post took 5 minutes to write. Many others I labored over for an hour flopped. Ride a hot topic.

- Luckforest (@lubinho_k) https://twitter.com/lubinho_k/status/2058243579022323755

A live topic in your niche can give you the four-figure view post. Ride it only if you have an add. Do not build the whole account on waves. Waves do not love you back on Tuesday.

Stop introducing yourself. Stop “new here” posts. At 600 you are a peer. Write like one. The reply block does not end at 500.

Video is optional at this size. It pays off more from 1k to 10k. Below that, a tight written explainer with one screenshot usually beats an average video. If fear of being on camera is the blocker, do not stall on it. Written posts will carry you to 1,000.

Drill · 15 minutes

Write the one sentence a stranger should remember. Put it next to the last seven originals. Anything that does not serve that sentence gets a kill vote. You need three kills or the sentence is a slogan you are not running.
