500–1,000: one format

1,000 means 20 unprompted repliers, a weekly post that can clear four-figure views, one sentence a stranger remembers.

If a format dies three times, it is dead. Do not invent a new persona at 780.

This post took 5 minutes to write. Many others I labored over for an hour flopped. Ride a hot topic.
