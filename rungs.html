Lesson 2 · Weeks 1–3

0–100: get seen

Originals get 8–40 views. Growth is replies that earn a profile click.

Column A: 25 peers (100–2,500). Column B: 10 borrow-reach accounts (5k–80k), reply in 15 minutes only with an add. Column C: 15 people who reply back. Talk to them every week.

Reply test: could this stand as its own post? Four shapes: number + condition; specific add; mini-story; precise question. 15–25 good replies/day. Answer author replies in 30 minutes.

Leave when: 100 niche followers, 8+ names in Column C, measurable profile visits. Random connect-thread mutuals do not count.

Lesson 3 · Weeks 3–6

100–500: get remembered

Four original jobs only, 4–6 a week: explainer, weekly scoreboard, proof artifact, opinion that travels. Goal-share posts are the strongest format in this band in ClimbX’s log.

Leave when: 15 repeat humans from memory, one recognizable weekly format.

Lesson 4 · Weeks 6–12

500–1,000: one format

Keep replying. Kill formats that die three times. Ride a live wave with your number attached. Milestone posts at 300 / 500 / 900.

1,000 is allowed to mean: 20 unprompted repliers, a weekly post that can clear four-figure views, and one sentence a stranger would remember.
