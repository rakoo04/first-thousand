Lesson 0 · 12 min

Rules of the game

X does not broadcast to your followers. Each post is a test: did this earn the next circle of viewers? Follower count is a weak prior.

The two currencies

Reach — impressions on replies and posts. Gravity — profile visits, follows, and the 15 humans who reply without being asked.

The ladder

Pass condition is not “1,000 followers.” It is 1,000 followers plus 15 people who would notice if you vanished.

Lesson 1 · Day 1, 40 min

Setup that converts a click

Bio: who it is for + what you are doing in public + optional 90-day bet. Face photo locked 90 days. Pin the bet, not the launch. Premium helps. New accounts: 48 hours of human behavior before volume.
