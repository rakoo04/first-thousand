Chapter 0 · 8 min · Orientation

This is a course. Treat it like one.

You can leave this chapter when you can say, in one sentence, who you are writing for, how many minutes you will spend a day, and which week you are starting.

What this is

A synthesis of public runs from people who published their numbers: Rob Hallam, Daniel Smidstrup (ClimbX), Luckforest, Nikita Bier’s ladder, and operator write-ups. It is not a leak of Hallam’s paid Attention Playbook (pre-sale as of 30 Sep 2026).

The unit of progress is not “I read a chapter.” The unit is a completed drill: a bio shipped, a 40-account list written down, 12 replies that could stand as posts.

How to study

  1. Read one chapter. Do the drill the same day. Do not stockpile chapters.
  2. Keep a single note titled “X log.” Three columns: date, what you shipped, what got a profile visit or a follow.
  3. Review Sunday. Kill one thing. Keep one thing. Refresh ten target accounts.
  4. If a chapter contradicts your last 14 days of data, trust the 14 days.

Who this is for

A founder, builder, or writer starting from 0–300 followers who will spend 45–60 minutes a day, six days a week. If you will not reply, this course will not work.

Who this is not for

Anyone shopping for a bot or a 48-hour screenshot. Luckforest took eight months and ~3,800 posts to cross 1,000. ClimbX hit 1,000 in 33 days only at 1–3 posts plus 50–100 replies a day.
