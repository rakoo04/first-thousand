Reply craft

Leave when you have a 40-account list and can write four reply shapes from memory.

Under 1,000, originals are homework. Replies are distribution.

1–3 posts a day and 50–100 replies the first 30 days.

— Daniel Smidstrup (@DanielSmidstrup) https://twitter.com/DanielSmidstrup/status/2066474894280274217
